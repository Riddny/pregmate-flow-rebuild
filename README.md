# Growth Audit — Pregmate

**Live:** https://riddny.github.io/pregmate-flow-rebuild/

Five ranked ideas for Pregmate's onboarding and paywall, plus a clickable prototype that shows each one in Pregmate's own design. Only the order of screens changes. The art direction does not.

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | What is there today | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 1 | Show her dates before the price | Last period, cycle length and period length are asked, then a loader, then the paywall. Nothing is shown back. | CR +10–20% |
| 2 | Name her goal on the paywall | The paywall says the same line to someone trying to conceive, someone pregnant and someone tracking her period. | CR +15–20% |
| 3 | Answer Skip with your free week | The 7-day trial on the $69.99 yearly plan sits behind a small toggle. Skip ends the sale. | ARPU +10–15% |
| 4 | Ask for notifications after the paywall | The reminder screen and the iOS pop-up come right before the loader and the price. | CR +10–15% |
| 5 | Put your 4.9 rating next to the price | The flow says "Trusted by millions" but never shows 4.9 from 13K ratings. | CR +10–15% |

## Grounding

Every price, rating, trial term and line of copy comes from Pregmate's own App Store listing and in-app screens. Dates in the prototype are counted from the sample answers with the standard calendar method and are estimates. Dark mode is an approximation.

Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps — not measured lift for Pregmate.
